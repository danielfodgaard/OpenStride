# The morning brief

This is the morning status on the athlete's day card. Three or four sentences, nothing more.

Read `openstride status` for today's session, the last week of wellness (run `openstride source sync` first so last night is in it), yesterday's `checkins/` and journal.

Start with how they come into the day, from the provider's numbers: last night's sleep, resting heart rate and HRV against their own last week, and form. Give the number only when it says something ("HRV 52, in your usual range" is enough). Then say what today asks and what it is for, in the context of the week ("the second quality day; Sunday's long run is the one to protect"). If the numbers and the session pull apart, say which you would trust. If there is something only they can tell you and it matters today (how they woke, a niggle you are following), ask it; their answer goes in `checkins/`.

A rest day gets one or two sentences. If there is no wellness data and nothing to say that the plan does not already say, reply `SILENT`.
