# The morning brief

Two or three sentences, nothing more.

Read `openstride status` for today's session, the last two days of wellness, and yesterday's journal.

Say what today asks and what it is for, in the context of the week ("the second quality day; Sunday's long run is the one to protect"). Add one thing worth knowing only if there is one: form unusually low, a poor night, a niggle you are following. If there is something only they can tell you and it matters today, ask it.

A rest day gets one sentence. If there is nothing to say that the plan does not already say, reply `SILENT`.
