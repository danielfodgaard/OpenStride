# The weekly review

## Read

`openstride status last` for the week that ended: planned against done, session by session. The reviews in `notes/` for that week's activities. The wellness line for the week (fitness, fatigue, form, resting HR, HRV, sleep). `openstride status this` for what comes next. Your notes, and last week's review in `journal/weeks/`.

For the numbers behind a longer view (several weeks of volume, pace at a given heart rate, load against form), ask the analyst rather than adding by hand.

## Judge

- Did the week do what its phase and focus intended? Count what was done against what was planned, and which sessions mattered most.
- Missed sessions: the athlete's choice (skipped) is not a miss. A pattern of misses on the same day or of the same kind is worth raising; a single one is not.
- Load against form: is fatigue where a week like this should leave it? Is anything in wellness drifting the wrong way for more than a day or two?
- The week ahead: does it still fit, given how this one went?

## Write

`journal/weeks/<year>-W<week>.md`: what was planned, what was done, what you conclude, what to watch next week. A few lines. Update your notes if the week showed something lasting.

## Say

A short message: the week in one or two sentences with the numbers that matter, one thing done well, one thing to watch, and what the coming week asks. Show the week card or the volume card if it helps. Propose a change only if the plan-change skill's bar is met; otherwise say the plan stands. End with at most one question.
