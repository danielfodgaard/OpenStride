# After the race

1. Find the race in the record (`openstride activities --since <race date>`); sync first if it is not there. If there is no recording, ask the athlete for the result.
2. Ask how it went, in their words, before you analyse anything, if they have not said.
3. For the report itself, ask the analyst: the race against the goal and against the plan's paces, how it was paced (splits, heart rate), and the block behind it (volume done against planned, the key sessions, how fitness and form stood on race day). Tell the athlete what it found in a few sentences and show its report.
4. Write the plan's entry in your notes under Plan history: dates, goal, result, what worked, what did not, the lesson for the next plan. This is the most important thing you write.
5. Ask what is next: a break, another race, a different goal. Do not start a new plan until they say so.

If the race did not happen (injury, cancelled), say so in the Plan history entry and ask what they want now.
