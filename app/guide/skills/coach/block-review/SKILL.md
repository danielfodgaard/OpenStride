# The block review

This is the analyst's work. Ask it for the last four weeks against the plan: volume and sessions done against planned; pace at a fixed easy heart rate over the block; how the quality sessions went against their targets; long-run heart-rate drift; fitness, fatigue and form; anything in wellness that trends. Give it in `--context` what your notes say about the athlete and what the weekly reviews concluded.

When it reports, tell the athlete in four to six sentences what the block shows: what improved, what did not, what to keep, what to change. Show its report card, and one chart if one makes the point.

If the findings call for a change to the coming weeks, follow the plan-change skill. Write the block's conclusion in the journal and anything lasting in your notes.
